1. Executive Summary & Key Wins

Fruitfulness, spiritual growth, and momentum across our youth ministry

Section 1

🌟 Internship Program Impact

Our students thoroughly enjoyed connecting with this summer’s interns, noting their relatability and effective engagement.

🙌 Junior High Leadership & Growth

Junior High Youth Minister Dylan Holland continues to build deep trust, love, and respect with our students. There is a large incoming 6th Grade class.

💡 Classroom Engagement Strategies

Direct, kind engagement methods have noticeably increased student attention and participation during classes.

🔥 Student Leadership Team (SLT) Launch

The SLT held its inaugural meeting on Sunday, August 23rd with full attendance. The team is focusing on outreach to students on the fringe and taking direct ownership of planning the Fall Retreat.

Summer Mentoring & Youth Engagement
